\subsection{A}
 
\begin{itemize}
\itemsep-.45em  
\item {AARON אַהֲרֹן, Ἀαρών  亚伦} \\
From the Hebrew name אַהֲרֹן ('Aharon), which is most likely of unknown Egyptian origin. Other theories claim a Hebrew derivation, and suggest meanings such as "high mountain" or "exalted". “住在山里的人、升高、开明的，开朗”
\item {ABADDON אֲבַדּוֹן 亚巴顿} \\
Means "ruin, destruction" in Hebrew. In Revelation, this is another name of the angel of the abyss.
\item {ABDIEL 押比疊 （代上 5:15）  עֲבְדִיאֵל} \\
Means "servant of God" in Hebrew. In OT, this is the name of a member of the tribe of Gad. 
\item {ABEDNEGO עֲבֵד־נְגוֹ} \\
Means "servant of Nebo" in Akkadian, Nebo being the Babylonian god of wisdom. In OT Abednego is the Babylonian name given to Azariah, one of the three men cast into a blazing furnace but saved from harm by God, as told in the Book of Daniel.
\item {ABEL הֶבֶל, Ἄβελעֲבֵד־נְגוֹ  亚伯} \\
From the Hebrew name הֶבֶל (Hevel) meaning "breath虚空”. In OT he is the second son of Adam and Eve, murdered out of envy by his brother Cain. 
\item {ABIAH אֲבִיָה} \\
Variant of ABIJAH, which also appears in the English Bible.
\item {ABIATHAR אֶבְיָתָר} \\
From Hebrew אֶבְיָתָר ('Evyatar) meaning "my father abounds" or "my father excels". In OT Abiathar was a high priest during the reign of King David.
\item {ABIDAN אֲבִידָן, Ἀβιδάν} \\
Means "my father is judge" in Hebrew. In the OT he is a Benjamite prince.
\item {ABIEL אֲבִיאֵל} \\
Means "God is my father" in Hebrew. This was the name of the grandfather of Saul in the OT.
\item {ABIGAIL 亚比该  אֲבִיגַיִל} \\
From the Hebrew name אֲבִיגָיִל ('Avigayil) meaning "my father is joy父亲的喜乐", derived from the roots אָב ('av) meaning "father" and גִּיל (gil) meaning "joy". In OT this is the name of Nabal's wife. After Nabal's death she became the third wife of King David. 1 Samuel 25:3,14,2 Samuel 3:3
\item {ABIHU אֲבִיהוּא 亚比户m}\\
Means "he is my father他是父亲" in Hebrew. This is the name of a son of Aaron in OT. He and his brother Nadab were killed by God because they presented him with unauthorized fire.

\item {ABIJAH אֲבִיָה 亚比雅 m and f}\\
Means "my father is YAHWEH耶和华是父亲光明之父" in Hebrew. In OT this is the name of several characters, both male and female, including the second king of Judah (also known as Abijam).
\item {ABIMAEL אֲבִימָאֵל m}\\
Meaning uncertain, though the first element is likely Hebrew אָב ('av) meaning "father". In OT he is listed as a descendant of Shem.
\item {ABIMELECH אֲבִימֶלֶך m}\\
Means "my father is king" in Hebrew. This is the name of several characters in OT including a king of Gerar who takes Abraham's wife Sarah, but is forced by God to give her back.
\item {ABIRAM אֲבִירָם 亚比兰}\\
Means "my father is exalted高处的父，我父受称颂" in Hebrew. In OT, Abiram is swallowed by an earthquake after rebelling against the leadership of Moses.
\item {ABISHAG אֲבִישַׁג }\\
Means "my father strays" in Hebrew. In OT Abishag is a young woman who tends King David in his old age.
\item {ABISHAI אֲבִישַׁי m}\\
Means "my father is a gift" in Hebrew. In OT he is one of King David's heroes.
\item {ABITAL אֲבִיטָל }\\
Means "my father is the night dew" in Hebrew. She is the fifth wife of David in OT
\item {ABNER אַבְנֵר }\\
Means "my father is a light" in Hebrew, from אָב ('av) meaning "father" and נֵר (ner) meaning "lamp, light". In OT, Abner was a cousin of Saul and the commander of his army. After he killed Asahel he was himself slain by Asahel's brother Joab. 
\item {ABRAHAM אַבְרָהָם 亚伯拉罕}\\
This name may be viewed either as meaning "father of many众人的父" in Hebrew or else as a contraction of ABRAM (1) and הָמוֹן (hamon) meaning "many, multitude". The biblical patriarch Abraham was originally named Abram but God changed his name (see Genesis 17:5). With his father Terah, he led his wife Sarah, his nephew Lot and their other followers from Ur into Canaan. He is regarded by Jews as being the founder of the Hebrews through his son Isaac and by Muslims as being the founder of the Arabs through his son Ishmael

\item {ABRAM (1) אַבְרָם }\\
Means "high father" in Hebrew. In OT God changed Abram's name to Abraham (see Genesis 17:5).

\item {ABSALOM אַבְשָׁלוֹם 押沙龙}
From the Hebrew name אַבְשָׁלוֹם ('Avshalom) meaning "my father is peace"“平安的父”, derived from אָב ('av) meaning "father" and שָׁלוֹם (shalom) meaning "peace". In the OT he is a son of King David. He avenged his sister Tamar by arranging the murder of her rapist, their half-brother Amnon. He later led a revolt against his father. While fleeing on the back of a mule he got his head caught in a tree and was killed by Joab.

\item {ACHAICUS Ἀχαϊκός }\\
Latinized form of the Greek name Ἀχαϊκός (Achaikos), which referred to the region in Greece called Ἀχαΐα (Achaia), situated on the northern coast of the Peloponnese. In the NT this is the name of a Corinthian Christian who aids Saint Paul.
\item {ACHAN עָכָן}\\
Possibly a variation of the Hebrew word עֲכָר ('akhar) meaning "trouble". In the OT, Achan is stoned to death because he steals forbidden items during the assault on Jericho.
\item {ACHIM (2) Ἀχείμ}\\
Possibly means "he will establish" in Hebrew. In the NT this name is listed as an ancestor of Jesus.
\item {ADAH עָדָה f}\\
Means "adornment" in Hebrew. This was the name of the wives of both Lamech and Esau in OT.
\item {ADALIA אֲדַלְיָא m}\\

Latinized form of the Greek name Τίμαιος (Timaios), derived from τιμάω (timao) meaning "to honour". This is the name of one of Plato's dialogues, featuring Timaeus and Socrates. Timaeus is also the name of a person mentioned briefly in the New Testament (Mark 10:46).
\item TIMEUS Τίμαιος m \\
Variant of TIMAEUS.
\item TIMON Τίμων m \\
Derived from Greek τιμάω (timao) meaning "to honour, to esteem". It appears briefly in the New Testament. 
\item TIMOTHY m English, \\
English form of the Greek name Τιμόθεος (Timotheos) meaning "honouring God", derived from τιμάω (timao) meaning "to honour" and θεός (theos) meaning "god". Saint Timothy was a companion of Paul on his missionary journeys and was the recipient of two of Paul's epistles that appear in the New Testament. He was of both Jewish and Greek ancestry. 
\item TIRAS תִּירָס m \\
Possibly means "desire" in Hebrew. Tiras is a grandson of Noah in the Old Testament.
\item TIRZAH תִּרְצָה f \\
From the Hebrew name תִּרְצָה (Tirtzah) meaning "favourable". Tirzah is the name of one of the daughters of Zelophehad in the Old Testament. It also occurs in the Old Testament as a place name, the early residence of the kings of the northern kingdom.
\item TITUS m\\
Roman praenomen, or given name, which is of unknown meaning, possibly related to Latin titulus "title of honour". It is more likely of Oscan origin, since it was borne by the legendary Sabine king Titus Tatius.
\item TOBIAH טוֹבִיָּה m \\
From the Hebrew name טוֹבִיָּה (Toviyyah) meaning "YAHWEH is good", from the elements טוֹב (tov) meaning "good" and יָה (yah) referring to the Hebrew God. This was the name of an Ammonite in the Book of Nehemiah in the Old Testament.
\item TOBIAS Τωβίας m \\
Greek form of TOBIAH. This is the name of the hero of the apocryphal Book of Tobit, which appears in many English versions of the Old Testament. 
\item TOBIT Τωβίθ m \\
From Greek Τωβίθ (Tobith), from the Hebrew name טוֹבִיה (Tovih) meaning "my good". 
\item TROPHIMUS m \\
From the Greek name Τρόφιμος (Trophimos) meaning "nourishing", a derivative of τροφή (trophe) meaning "nourishment, food". In the New Testament this is the name of a companion of Paul.
\item TRYPHENA Τρύφαινα f \\
From the Greek name Τρύφαινα (Tryphaina), derived from Greek τρυφή (tryphe) meaning "softness, delicacy". This name is mentioned briefly in the New Testament.
\item TRYPHOSA Τρυφῶσα f \\
Derived from Greek τρυφή (tryphe) meaning "softness, delicacy". In the New Testament this name is mentioned briefly as belonging to a companion of Tryphena.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{U}
\begin{itemize}
\itemsep-.5em 
\item URBAN m \\
From the Latin name Urbanus meaning "city dweller". This name is mentioned briefly in one of Paul's epistles in the New Testament. It was subsequently borne by eight popes.
\item URI אוּרִי m \\
Means "my light" in Hebrew. This is the name of the father of Bezalel in the Old Testament.
\item URIAH אוּרִיָה 乌利亚 \\
From the Hebrew name אוּרִיָה ('Uriyah) meaning "YAHWEH is my light神是我的亮光”", from the roots אוּר ('ur) meaning "light, flame" and יָה (yah) referring to the Hebrew God. In the Old Testament this is the name of a Hittite warrior in King David's army, the first husband of Bathsheba. David desired Bathsheba so he placed Uriah in the forefront of battle so he would be killed.
\item URIEL אוּרִיאֵל m \\
From the Hebrew name אוּרִיאֵל ('Uri'el) meaning "God is my light", from אוּר ('ur) meaning "light, flame" and אֵל ('el) meaning "God". Uriel is an archangel in Hebrew tradition. He is mentioned only in the Apocrypha, for example in the Book of Enoch where he warns Noah of the coming flood.
\item URIJAH אוּרִיָה  \\
Form of URIAH used in some English versions of the Old Testament (for a character in the Book of Jeremiah).
\item UZZI עֻזִּי m \\
Means "my power" in Hebrew. This is the name of several minor characters in the Old Testament.
\item UZZIAH עֻזִּיָּה 乌西雅 \\
Means "my power is YAHWEH神的力量" in Hebrew, from the roots עֹז ('oz) meaning "strength, power" and יָה (yah) referring to the Hebrew God. This is the name of several Old Testament characters including a king of Judah.
\item UZZIEL עֻזִּיאֵל \\
Means "my power is God" in Hebrew, from the roots עֹז ('oz) meaning "strength, power" and אֵל ('el) meaning "God". This is the name of several minor characters in the Old Testament.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{V}
\begin{itemize}
\itemsep-.5em 
\item VASHTI וַשְׁתִּי 瓦实提 \\
Possibly means "thread" in Hebrew, but it is most likely of Persian origin. In the Old Testament this is the name of the first wife of King Ahasuerus of Persia before he marries Esther.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Z}
\begin{itemize}
\itemsep-.5em 

\item ZACCAI זַכָּי m \\
From the Hebrew name זַכָּי (Zakkai) meaning "pure". This is the name of a minor character in the Old Testament.
\item ZACCHAEUS Ζακχαῖος m \\
From Ζακχαῖος (Zakchaios), the Greek form of ZACCAI. In the New Testament he is a tax collector in Jericho who gives half his possessions to charity.
\item ZACHARIAH m \\
Variant of ZECHARIAH. This spelling is used in the King James Version of the Old Testament to refer to one of the kings of Israel (called Zechariah in other versions).
\item ZACHARIAS Ζαχαρίας m \\
Greek form of ZECHARIAH. This form of the name is used in most English versions of the New Testament to refer to the father of John the Baptist. 
\item ZACHARY m\\
Usual English form of ZACHARIAS, used in some English versions of the New Testament. 
\item ZADOK צָדוֹק m \\
Means "righteous" in Hebrew. This is the name of several characters in the Old Testament, most notably the high priest of Israel during the reigns of David and Solomon. Solomon was anointed by Zadok.
\item ZALMON צַלְמוֹן m \\
Means "shady" in Hebrew. This is the name of one of David's mighty men in the Old Testament.
\item ZARAH זֵרַח m \\
Form of ZERAH used in some translations of the Bible.
\item ZEBADIAH זְבַדְיָה m \\
Means "YAHWEH has bestowed" in Hebrew. This was the name of several Old Testament characters.
\item ZEBEDEE Ζεβεδαῖος m \\
From Ζεβεδαῖος (Zebedaios), the Greek form of ZEBADIAH used in the New Testament, where it refers to the father of the apostles James and John.
\item ZEBULON זְבוּלֻן m \\
Variant of ZEBULUN.
\item ZEBULUN זְבוּלֻן m \\
Possibly derived from Ugartic zbl meaning "prince". In the Old Testament Zebulun is the tenth son of Jacob (his sixth son by Leah) and the ancestor of one of the twelve tribes of Israel. Genesis 30:20 implies two different roots for the name: זָבַל (zaval) meaning "to dwell" and זֵבֵד (zeved) meaning "gift, dowry". These are probably only folk etymologies.
\item ZECHARIAH זְכַרְיָה 撒迦利亚 \\
From the Hebrew name זְכַרְיָה (Zekharyah) meaning "YAHWEH remembers耶和华纪念", from זָכַר (zakhar) meaning "to remember" and יָה (yah) referring to the Hebrew God. This is the name of many characters in the Old Testament, including the prophet Zechariah, the author of the Book of Zechariah. The name also appears in the New Testament belonging to the father of John the Baptist, who was temporarily made dumb because of his disbelief. He is regarded as a saint by Christians. In some versions of the New Testament his name is spelled in the Greek form Zacharias or the English form Zachary. 
\item ZEDEKIAH צִדְקִיָּהוּ 西底家又名玛探雅 \\
From the Hebrew name צִדְקִיָּהוּ (Tzidqiyyahu) meaning "justice of YAHWEH耶和华是义，神的公义", from צֶדֶק (tzedeq) meaning "justice" and יָה (yah) referring to the Hebrew God. In the Old Testament this is the name of the last king of Judah.
\item ZELOPHEHAD צְלָפְחָד m \\
Possibly means either "first born" or "shadow from terror" in Hebrew. In the Old Testament, Zelophehad is a man who dies while the Israelites are wandering in the wilderness, leaving five daughters as heirs.
\item ZEPHANIAH צְפַנְיָה 西番雅 \\
From the Hebrew name צְפַנְיָה (Tzefanyah) meaning "YAHWEH has hidden耶和华隐匿保护、珍爱", derived from צָפַן (tzafan) meaning "to hide" and יָה (yah) referring to the Hebrew God. This is the name of one of the twelve minor prophets of the Old Testament, the author of the Book of Zephaniah.
\item ZERAH זֵרַח m \\
Means "dawning, shining" in Hebrew. This is the name of a son of Judah and the twin of Perez in the Old Testament.
\item Zerubbabel זְרֻבָּבֶל 所罗巴伯 \\
Possibly means "conceived and born in Babylon生在巴比伦" from a contraction of either Assyrian-Babylonian Zəru Bābel "seed of Babylon" or Hebrew זְרוּעַ בָּבֶל (Zərua‘ Bāvel) "the one sown of Babylon". In the Old Testament he led the first group of Jews out of captivity in Babylon in the first year of the Persian king Cyrus.
\item ZIBA (2) צִיבָה m\\
Means "station" in Hebrew. This was the name of a servant of Saul in the Old Testament.
\item ZIBIAH צִבְיָה f \\
Means "gazelle" or "doe" in Hebrew. In the Old Testament this is the name of the mother of King Joash of Judah.
\item ZILLAH צִלָּה f \\
Means "shade" in Hebrew. In the Old Testament she is the second wife of Lamech.
\item ZILPAH זִלְפָּה f \\
Means "frailty" in Hebrew. In the Old Testament this is the name of the handmaid who was given to Jacob by Leah.
\item ZIMRI זִמְרִי 心利 \\
Means "my praise" or "my music唱歌出名，歌唱者" in Hebrew. This is the name of a king of Israel in the Old Testament. He ruled for only seven days, when he was succeeded by the commander of the army Omri.
\item ZION צִיוֹן m \\
From the name of a citadel that was in the center of Jerusalem. Zion is also used to refer to a Jewish homeland and to heaven.
\item ZIPPORAH צִפּוֹרָה f\\
From the Hebrew name צִפּוֹרָה (Tzipporah), derived from צִפּוֹר (tzippor) meaning "bird". In the Old Testament this is the name of the Midianite wife of Moses. She was the daughter of the priest Jethro.
\item ZURIEL צוּרִיאֵל m\\
Means "my rock is God" in Hebrew. In the Old Testament this name is borne by a chief of the Merarite Levites at the time of the Exodus.
\end{itemize}
